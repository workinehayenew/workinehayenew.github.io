I am a PhD candidate in economics at the Economy and Methodology Group, Oslo Business School, Oslo Metropolitan University (OsloMet), Norway. My research is in development economics and the economics of conflict. I study how armed conflict and economic change affect violence within the household, including intimate partner violence and violent discipline of children, with most of my work set in Ethiopia and Sub-Saharan Africa.

My work combines large household surveys such as the DHS, MICS and LSMS with georeferenced data on conflict events and climate, and uses applied microeconometric methods to estimate causal effects. My paper on conflict exposure and intimate partner violence in Ethiopia, with Andreas Kotsadam, Charlotte Ringdal and Espen Villanger, is published in *Social Science & Medicine*.

Before starting my PhD, I was a research assistant at the Policy Studies Institute in Addis Ababa and a lecturer in economics at Debre Berhan University and Wachemo University.

Research interests
------
Development economics, applied microeconometrics, economics of conflict, gender and intra-household economics, intimate partner violence, violence against children.
